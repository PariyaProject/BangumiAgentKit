## Inference, safe variant, and disposition

“Only non-TV” is a universal negative claim: it requires evidence that all relevant recent voice relations were observed and none are TV. The current v0 relation response has no documented pagination/total, and the capability deliberately samples relation rows and subject details before classifying dates/platforms. A missing non-TV row, an unknown platform, or a partial result therefore cannot establish either “only non-TV” or “no recent TV work.”

A useful positive-only salvage is narrower: **“In the bounded current sample of this person's voice credits, did we observe at least one anime subject whose listed platform is explicitly recognized as non-TV, using its subject air/release date in the last six calendar months?”** A suitable future fixed call would use one known `personId`, `kind=voice`, `media=anime`, `windowMonths=6`, and explicit relation/detail/row caps (candidate 48/16/8). A positive answer can name only returned qualifying rows and their source platform/date. No qualifying row, an unknown platform, or partial coverage must be reported as “not established in this sample,” never as “no TV work” or “only non-TV.” `media=all` could broaden the returned subject types, but the current TV/non-TV heuristic was built for anime platform filtering; it does not provide an exhaustive cross-type TV partition.
