# S01: Annual TV anime voice-credit count contract

**Scenario:** “某声优过去三年每年配音多少部 TV 动画？”

**Checked:** 2026-10-08

## Measure and source fields

For `kind=voice`, the current activity service reads `GET /v0/persons/{person_id}/characters`; each `PersonCharacter` supplies a character ID, linked `subject_id`/`subject_type`, names, and an optional raw `staff` label. The separate `/persons/{person_id}/subjects` operation returns related subject rows (`id`, `type`, `staff`, `eps`, names) and is used by this service for `kind=staff`, not for the voice path. Both OpenAPI operations return bare arrays and define no query pagination or total field. Thus the observed graph is the array returned by the current v0 operation; the contract does not expose a historical snapshot or a paging completeness check. [Official person operations](https://github.com/bangumi/server/blob/master/openapi/v0.yaml#L3794-L3882), [PersonCharacter and RelatedSubject schemas](https://github.com/bangumi/server/blob/master/openapi/v0.yaml#L3231-L3272) [and](https://github.com/bangumi/server/blob/master/openapi/v0.yaml#L4099-L4130). Local mirror: `openapi/upstream/v0.yaml:L836-L890`, `L3231-L3272`, `L4099-L4130`; voice/staff path selection and relation mapping: `packages/bangumi-core/src/services/person-activity-service.ts:L580-L637`.

Those relation rows do not carry the work’s date or platform. The service therefore fetches bounded `GET /v0/subjects/{subject_id}` details and maps official `Subject.type`, `date` (described as an air date in `YYYY-MM-DD`), and `platform`. [Official subject detail operation and schema](https://github.com/bangumi/server/blob/master/openapi/v0.yaml#L2804-L2834) [and fields](https://github.com/bangumi/server/blob/master/openapi/v0.yaml#L3572-L3628). Local mapping/hydration: `packages/bangumi-core/src/services/person-activity-service.ts:L656-L690`, `L754-L787`; `packages/bangumi-core/src/services/subject-service.ts:L126-L147`.

For `media=tv`, the current implementation first requires `type=anime`, then classifies platform text: `TV`, `television`, or `电视` is included; movie/theatrical, OVA, web, and music markers are excluded; missing or unrecognized text is unknown and makes coverage partial. [Official `SubjectType` marks `2` as anime and `SubjectAnimeCategory` defines `1` as TV](https://github.com/bangumi/server/blob/master/openapi/v0.yaml#L3806-L3824) [and](https://github.com/bangumi/server/blob/master/openapi/v0.yaml#L3868-L3894). The current person-activity classifier uses the platform field, not that category enum: `packages/bangumi-core/src/services/person-activity-service.ts:L187-L193`, `L735-L752`.

## Window, yearly buckets, and truthful claims

`windowMonths=36` means the current calendar month plus the 35 preceding calendar months. The start is the first day of the first month; the end is the as-of date, inclusive. On 2026-10-08 that is **2023-11-01 through 2026-10-08**: 2023 covers Nov–Dec, 2024 and 2025 are full calendar years, and 2026 covers Jan 1–Oct 8. This is not the last three completed calendar years. The service currently emits monthly buckets; annual buckets must group by the work’s official first-air date/year. Count distinct `subject_id` per year (“unique TV anime works”), not relation rows or characters, since multiple character credits can point to one subject. Local window/summary implementation: `packages/bangumi-core/src/services/person-activity-service.ts:L107-L125`, `L375-L402`; result contract: `packages/bangumi-core/src/models/person-activity.ts:L43-L64`.

These are counts by Bangumi’s current person→character→subject links and each subject’s first-air date, not the year when voice recording happened, a count of recording sessions, or a complete career history. Each request caps relation selection at 80 by default / 120 maximum, subject-detail requests at 32 / 48, and returned rows at 40 / 60; subject/person/relation responses also have a 1 MiB byte limit. Relation and detail caps use deterministic even sampling. Missing dates, failed details, and unknown TV platforms also degrade coverage. When state is `partial`, yearly numbers can only be described as **works observed in this run**; the sample is not year-stratified, so no bucket is independently complete. A zero observed count under partial coverage is not evidence of zero works. The monthly summary is computed before the returned-row cap, but its counts remain limited by relation/detail sampling. Local caps, coverage, warnings, and claim limitations: `packages/bangumi-core/src/services/person-activity-service.ts:L35-L42`, `L651-L690`, `L808-L848`, `L858-L918`, `L1002-L1055`; public contract: `docs/tool-catalog.json:L1284-L1351`.

If coverage is complete, phrase the result as a count in the **current Bangumi v0 relation records**, assigned to years by subject first-air date and TV-filtered by the current platform rule. Keep the partial first/last-year date ranges visible. Even a complete run describes present source records and first-air dates, not historical snapshots or actual voice-work timing.
